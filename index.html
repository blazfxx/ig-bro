🎭 Speak Like…

Type anything, pick a person, and it gets rewritten in their texting style — using their real word choices, tics, and habits mined from the chat
Tip: press Enter to translate. Hit Translate again for a different random take.

How it works

Each person's profile stores: how often they capitalize, their question/exclamation/CAPS rates, preferred spellings (“you”→“u”, “because”→“bc”…), signature tics (“bruh”, “lol”, “nu uh”), word-stretching habits (sooo), and favorite emojis — all measured from their real messages. Your text is re-spelled, re-cased and decorated to match those measured probabilities.
